## chapter 2
goal: error-free sentences
#### 1、汽车和飞机的尾气导致空气污染（主+谓+宾）
The emissions from vehicles and planes can cause air pollution.
#### 2、现代科技让富人更加容易积累财富（主+谓+宾+宾补）
Modern technoogy has allowed rich people to accumulate wealth more easily.
#### 3、电脑科技可以给人们相互交流的机会（主+谓+宾+直宾）
Computer technology gives people opportunities to communicate with each other.
#### 4、那些重犯需要被送到监狱服刑。(主+被动语态)
Those serious offenders should be sent to prison.
#### 5、学费一直在上涨。（主+不及物动词）
The tuition fee has been rising.
#### 6、长时间工作已经成为公司里的惯例。（主+系+表）
Working long hours has become the norm in companies.
#### 7、很多应聘者竞争有限的职位。（there be 句型）
There are many job applicants competing for limited positions.
#### 8、创造幸福、繁荣的社会将会是很难的（it充当形式主语或宾语）
It woule be difficult to create a happy, prosperous society.
#### 9、一些国家的人如果犯罪会面临长期服刑。（状语从句）
People in some countries have to face a long prison term if they commit crimes.
#### 10、一些人认为严厉的惩罚可以减少犯罪（名词性从句）
Some people argue that harsh punishments can help deter crime.
#### 11、减税对于那些生活拮据的人有好处。（定语从句）
Tax reductions are benefical to those who live on a tight budget.
